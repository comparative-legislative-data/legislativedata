Robin Rigg Offshore Wind Farm (Navigation and Fishing) (Scotland) Bill

 

Outcome: E

A Bill to regulate matters relating to navigation and fishing in connection with the provision of an offshore wind farm in navigable waters within the Solway Firth; and for connected purposes.

Current Status of the Bill
