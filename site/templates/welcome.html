Scottish Parliament

Data about legislation in the Scottish Parliament

Every bill introduced in the Scottish Parliament since 1999: when each stage ended, what the Parliament did with it, and where each fact came from. The site is in beta, and the data is open to researchers with an account.

Data

Every bill, one line each, to narrow by session, type, outcome and title. Open a bill to see everything recorded about it and the source of each fact. The whole dataset downloads as one zip, with a readme and a codebook. The methodology notes, the sources and their terms, and what has changed are alongside.

Insights

Charts drawn from the same data, each with the bills behind it and the working that produced it. They are being built.

Apply for an account, and sign in

Every application is read and decided by hand. Once approved, you sign in with a code sent to your email. There are no passwords.

Privacy

What the site holds about you, which is as little as it can work with.
