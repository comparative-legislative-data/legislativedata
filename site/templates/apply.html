What this form keeps

  • Your email address, name, title and position, and the date you applied.
  • Whether your application was approved, and when.
  • Nothing else: not your IP address, not your browser, and nothing about your visit.
  • An approved application is kept while you have an account. A refused one is deleted once you have been told. To have yours deleted, write to comparativelegislativedata@gmail.com.
  • Emails from the site, including the decision on your application, are sent through Resend, which therefore sees your email address.
