# Wording: what every page carries once there is data

For the owner. Strand 1, item 5 of `docs/PHASE-2.md`. **Drafted 18 September
2026, not yet agreed.** Every word a reader will see is below, in full. The
download's own wording (its readme and suggested citation) is strand 2, and a
chart's is strand 3.

The date shown is the copy's date, written out; 18 September 2026 is today's
copy. Credit lines are the ones agreed on 18 September and held in the copy's
`terms` file, so a page takes them from there and never from this document.

---

## 1. At the foot of every page

Replaces today's "No data published yet" once the first data page is live.
Every page, signed in or not, data or not.

> Data as at 18 September 2026 · What has changed
>
> legislativedata.org is an independent research resource. It is not the
> Scottish Parliament's, and the Parliament has not endorsed it.

"What has changed" links to the list of changes. The second line is the
sentence the Parliament's licence requires: its data may not be used in a way
that suggests official status or endorsement.

## 2. At the top of every page showing data

Beside the heading, in the muted type settled on 15 September, so that a
screenshot of the table keeps its date. Then one line under it.

> Data as at 18 September 2026
>
> Accurate as at that date. A record it was taken from may since have been
> corrected, and earlier versions are not kept. See what has changed.

## 3. At the bottom of every page showing data: sources and licence

One line for each set of terms the page's figures use, in the order of the
`terms` file, and always the last line, since every page carries our own
work. A page drawing on all four shows all four; a page drawing only on the
Parliament and our work shows two.

> ## Sources and licence
>
> Values from the Scottish Parliament: Contains information licensed under the
> Scottish Parliament Copyright Licence
>
> Values from legislation.gov.uk: Contains public sector information licensed
> under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
>
> Values from the Supreme Court: Contains public sector information licensed
> under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
>
> Everything else: Contains data from legislativedata.org, licensed under CC BY
> 4.0. Stage 1 and Stage 2 dates compiled for Steven MacGregor, 'Does
> government dominate the legislative process?' (PhD thesis, University of
> Stirling, 2021).
>
> This data is provided as it is, with no warranty. legislativedata.org is not
> responsible for what anyone does with it.
>
> What each source allows, and does not, is on the Sources page.

Each licence's name in its line links to the licence. "Values from the
Scottish Parliament" means every value whose recorded source is under the
Parliament's terms, the PhD dataset and Manual included, as settled.

## 4. On the Sources page: the terms

The reference page that lists the sources is built in strand 2. This is the
part of it that gives the terms: an opening paragraph, then one block per line
of the `terms` file, built from it.

> ## Terms of use
>
> Every value in this data names its source. A value from one of the sources
> below stays under that source's terms, and its restrictions pass to you with
> it. Everything else is our own work, under CC BY 4.0. When you use the data,
> include the credit line for each source your use draws on.
>
> This data is provided as it is, with no warranty. legislativedata.org is not
> responsible for what anyone does with it.
>
> ### Scottish Parliament
>
> **Covers:** Parliament API; SPICe legislation factsheet; Official Report; Bill
> document; PhD dataset; Manual; Parliament's bill page; SPICe dates factsheet
>
> **Licence:** Scottish Parliament Copyright Licence
>
> **Credit line:** Contains information licensed under the Scottish Parliament
> Copyright Licence
>
> **What it does not allow, in its own words:** This licence does not grant you
> any right to use the information in a way that suggests any official status
> or that the SPCB endorses you or your use of the information. This licence
> does not allow you to use published material provided in any format in
> connection with party political purposes or in connection with advertising
> endorsement.
>
> **Terms read on** 18 September 2026, at parliament.scot/about/copyright.

The other three blocks follow the same pattern from their lines of the `terms`
file; for our own work, "What it does not allow" reads "None." and there is no
"Terms read on" line.
