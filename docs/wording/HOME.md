# Wording: the home page

For the owner. **Draft, 19 September 2026, not agreed.** Every word of the
page at `/`, which everyone sees, signed in or not. It replaces the Phase 1
page ("A research resource, in preparation. Nothing is published here yet."),
which was to be looked at when the table went live
(`docs/STRAND-2-SHARED-PAGE-PARTS.md`, "Not in this item") and was not.

It carries no figure: a signed-out visitor sees no data, and the page would
otherwise need its own date and credit lines. Each heading links to its page.

## The page, in full

> SCOTTISH PARLIAMENT
>
> # Data about legislation in the Scottish Parliament
>
> Every bill introduced in the Scottish Parliament since 1999: when each
> stage ended, what the Parliament did with it, and where each fact came
> from. The site is in beta, and the data is open to researchers with an
> account.
>
> ## Data
>
> Every bill, one line each, to narrow by session, type, outcome and title.
> Open a bill to see everything recorded about it and the source of each
> fact. The whole dataset downloads as one zip, with a readme and a
> codebook. The methodology notes, the sources and their terms, and what has
> changed are alongside.
>
> ## Insights
>
> Charts drawn from the same data, each with the bills behind it and the
> working that produced it. They are being built.
>
> ## Apply for an account, and sign in
>
> Every application is read and decided by hand. Once approved, you sign in
> with a code sent to your email. There are no passwords.
>
> ## Privacy
>
> What the site holds about you, which is as little as it can work with.
