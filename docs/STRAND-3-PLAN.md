## Not settled yet, with a proposal for each

These are about the page and the parts every chart shares. They have to
be settled before the first chart is built, because the first chart
builds them.

### A. The shape of the Insights page

Nothing says yet what order the six come in, or whether it is one long
page. Each chart brings a table, a CSV and its working, so six of them is
longer than the Data page was when it went into tabs.

**Proposed: the same shape as the Data page.**
- At the top, the date and the date statement, as on Data.
- Beneath, **the headline figures** (thought 1), always in view, as the
  download is on Data.
- Then **three tabs**:
  - **Outcomes**: the outcomes chart (thought 2), its table beneath
    (thought 3).
  - **Time taken**: how long bills take (thought 4), then the quickest and
    slowest (thought 6). This is your grouping of the time charts.
  - **When bills are introduced**: the quarters (thought 5).
- A tab appears only once its charts are built.

### B. One frame for every chart

What each chart looks like, top to bottom, so all six look alike. What was
settled on 16–17 September is the figure first, then the rule and notes,
then the working folded away. This fills in where everything else sits:

1. The title, and the line beneath saying what the chart shows.
2. The dropdowns.
3. The line saying what hovering shows, as on the mock-ups.
4. The chart, with its sources and date inside its frame, so a screenshot
   or a saved image carries them. Hovering gives the figures.
5. The rule in one sentence, with the notes it rests on, each linking to
   the note on the Data page.
6. The explanations beneath, as the mock-ups have them (how a figure is
   worked out, Session 7, the traps the notes cover).
7. The table of every number the chart is showing, for the choices made.
8. "Download these figures" (the CSV).
9. "Show the working", folded away.

The sentence for a reader who cannot see the chart is read out by their
screen reader. The mock-up of thought 2 also shows it on the page, headed
"Description"; whether it stays visible is for you (see the checklist).

### C. From a figure to its bills

The table of every bill can be narrowed by one session, one type, one
outcome and words in the title. That reaches some figures, but not most:

- **"Not passed"**, grouped, is several outcomes at once.
- **Government Bills** on the charts include the Forth Crossing Bill; on
  the table it is a Hybrid Bill, so it would be missing.
- **An average of time taken, or a quarter of a session**, is not
  something the table can be narrowed to at all.

**Proposed:** each chart's working also hands back, for every figure, the
bills it counted. Clicking a figure opens the table of every bill showing
exactly those bills, with a line above saying which figure they are
("The 42 bills behind: Session 3, Government Bills, Passed") and a way back
to all bills. It is one addition to the table, built with the first chart
and used by all six. It adds nothing a reader can use to build their own
selection, so it stays clear of the playground.

### D. Seeing all six before building the parts they share

The frame, the dropdowns and the route to the bills have to suit all six
charts. But only thought 2's mock-up is accepted, and all six mock-ups
predate the Data page's tabs, pop-up and look.

**Proposed:** before anything is built, one mock-up of the whole Insights
page, in the site's own look, with all six in place: thought 2 as
accepted, the other five as they stood on 17 September, marked "not
agreed". It is for settling A, B and C by seeing them, not for agreeing
the five. Each of those still gets its own round of changes and its
write-up just before it is built.

## The order, session by session

Roughly the ten to twelve sessions `PHASE-2.md` sized. Every build has
its rehearsal and written undo, and is tested on the live site.

1. **This session.** This plan agreed. The whole-page mock-up (D), and A,

3. **Thought 2 built, with everything shared.** Probably two sessions:
   - **the figures**: the working, and the bills behind each figure,
     worked out by the refresh into the copy, with the copy's check

   Its closure test written.

   test run, then the next chart's mock-up changes, write-up agreed,
   built, and its test written.

   and run by one that built none of the phase.

## What the mock-ups can do: the checklist

Read from the mock-up page's own code, 19 September. **Everything here is
carried into the build** unless it is marked, and a mark means only that it
needs your OK before it goes. Each chart's closure test checks its list.

### Thought 1, the headline figures
- The number of bills as the large figure, with a line of what it covers.
- Four tiles: bills passed (with the share, and Acts against passed);
  a typical bill's days to Stage 3; bills before the Parliament now; the
  share passed by who introduced them, each "of" its number.
- The line that every figure opens to the bills behind it, and the date
  last checked against the sources.
- Four explanations beneath, naming M1, M2, M4, M5, M6, M13.

### Thought 2, the outcomes chart
- For each session, a bar per type of bill, stacked by outcome, with the
  count on each part big enough to hold it and the number of bills above
  each bar ("–" where none was introduced).
- A second panel, every session together, on its own scale.
- Sessions labelled with their years; types named along the bottom.
- Dropdowns: "Passed or not" or the full breakdown; the Forth Crossing
  Bill counted as a government bill or shown as a Hybrid Bill.
- Hovering a bar: the session and type, bills introduced, and each outcome
  with its count and share; "None introduced" where there were none.
- A legend of the outcomes, a colour each.
- The Session 7 line; the description; the notes it rests on, each with
  its title.
- Redraws for dark and light.
- **For your OK:** the description shown on the page (B, above).

### Thought 3, the outcomes table
- Dropdowns: type of bill (all, or one; Hybrid added when shown on its
  own); "Passed or not" or the full breakdown; the Forth Crossing Bill.
- Columns: session with its years, length in years, bills introduced,
  bills a year, then each outcome as a count and a share, with its colour
  beside the heading.
- An all-sessions row; "so far" beside Session 7's length and bills a
  year; "–" where none was introduced, "0" in grey, "<1%" for a share that
  rounds to nothing.
- Three explanations beneath.

### Thought 4, how long bills take
- A line across the sessions, each point labelled with its days.
- Dropdowns: type (all, or one of four); stretch (introduction to Stage
  3, to Stage 1, Stage 1 to 2, Stage 2 to 3, Stage 3 to Royal Assent);
  mean or median, mean by default; bills that passed or every bill that
  reached the end of the stretch.
- A fifth dropdown that appears only for Stage 3 to Royal Assent: bills
  stopped before Royal Assent included or left out.
- A dashed line for every session together, labelled with its figure.
- Hovering: bills, median, mean, shortest and longest; "No bill has
  finished this stretch yet" for Session 7, which is left blank.
- Six explanations beneath.

### Thought 5, when bills are introduced
- Dropdowns: type (government by default, all, or one of four); and the
  picture, of three.
- Four tiles, Sessions 1 to 6: bills; share in the last quarter against
  an even 25%, with the even-spread test; how far through on average
  against 50%; the change per session, with its test.
- Picture 1: a bar per session split into its quarters, each labelled with
  its share, and dashed guides at 25, 50 and 75%; hovering gives each
  quarter's count and share.
- Picture 2: a line per session of the share introduced so far against
  how far through, beside the straight line of an even rate; hovering at
  any point gives every session's share by then; a session can be picked
  out, and switched off in the legend.
- Picture 3: every bill as a dot on its session's row, coloured by
  quarter; hovering gives its title, type, bill number, date introduced,
  and how far through and which quarter.
- The table: bills and each quarter by session, how far through on
  average, half the bills in by, the even-spread test ("–" with a reason
  where not run), a Sessions 1 to 6 row, and "so far" for Session 7.
- A hint line that changes with the picture; seven explanations beneath.

### Thought 6, the quickest and slowest
- Two lists side by side. Dropdowns: type (all, or one of four); timed to
  Stage 3 or to Royal Assent.
- Each bill: place (shared places shown =6, every bill tied for tenth
  kept), title, type, session, Act or bill number, introduced and end
  dates, days, and a bar against the longest in view.
- Flags: emergency bill; referred to the Supreme Court; reintroduced.
- A line saying out of how many bills, and that with twenty or fewer a
  bill can be in both lists.
- Six explanations beneath.
